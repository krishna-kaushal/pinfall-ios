How to play: tap a metal pin to pull it out. Water cools lava (both vanish in steam). Poison kills goblins. Poison turns water it touches into poison, and lava destroys gold it touches. Gold wins only when 30+ pieces reach the hero's chamber and the chamber is clear of goblins, lava and poison — some levels need gold from two pockets. Wrong pin? You'll find out fast. Retry is instant.
